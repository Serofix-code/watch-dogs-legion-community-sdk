# Camera calibration — 2026-08-14

Status: **negative result / camera still unknown**.

An automatic differential scan used injected horizontal and vertical camera movement with alternating movement/stop Changed and Unchanged phases. Physical keyboard and mouse input were suppressed during calibration, and the user did not provide movement input.

The run produced 85 staged exports and reduced the set to one transient address. The survivor varied only from approximately `367.0000` to `367.0078` despite repeated substantial movement on both axes. It was present throughout horizontal and vertical stages and did not exhibit meaningful axis or direction correlation.

Conclusion: forcing horizontal and vertical candidates into one scalar favors timers, render jitter, and other always-changing values. The survivor is not a validated camera field and must not be written.

Next method:

1. Preserve independent horizontal and vertical candidate sets.
2. Require signed reversal when injected direction reverses.
3. Rank horizontal/vertical candidates that are adjacent within the same allocation.
4. Search for position, Euler/quaternion, matrix, and FOV clusters.
5. Reproduce across sessions and validate read-only before any reversible nudge test.

Local raw CSV logs are intentionally not committed.
